Questions

Not answered here? Send an order with your question. It's free, and we'll reply in your order chat.

How do I place an order?

Fill out the order form on this page and press Send my order. A private chat with BotForge opens right away. Your quote shows up there, and you can message us any time. No account needed. Bookmark the chat so you can come back to it.

Which platforms do you build for?

Discord and Minecraft are our main focus, and we also build for Telegram, Twitch, Slack, Reddit and websites. If your platform allows bots, describe what you need and we'll tell you in your chat whether we can build it.

When do I pay?

After you accept the quote in your order chat. Sending an order is free.

How long does a bot take?

Simple bots usually take 2 to 3 days. Your quote gives a date based on what you ask for.

Do I have to host the bot myself?

No. We can host it for you, so there's nothing to download or keep running. If you'd rather host it yourself, ask us about getting the source.

Can you build something that isn't listed?

Yes. Tick "Something that isn't on these lists", describe it in the form, and we'll work it out with you.

Can I change the bot after it launches?

Yes. Tell us what you'd like changed and we'll quote it before we start.

What do you need from me?

A name for the bot, what it should do, any commands you already have in mind, the platform it runs on (plus your server, channel or site if there is one), and your budget. A Discord username or email is optional, but it helps if you ever lose your chat link.

Privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information BotForge (botforgeservices.dev) collects when you use this site, and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • What you type into the order form: your bot's name, the platforms and features you pick, the package, hosting and budget, your server, channel or site, what the bot should do and any commands
  • Your Discord username or email, if you choose to add one (it's optional)
  • The messages you send in your order chat

Your order and chat are private. Only you and the BotForge team can see them. Anyone who has your chat link can open the chat, so don't share it.

We don't store your IP address. We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser saves your chat link in its local storage so you can get back to your chat. You can clear it in your browser settings at any time.

Other services this site uses

  • Cloudflare runs our order chat and stores your order and messages for us. Cloudflare processes IP addresses to deliver and protect the service, under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Discord: we get a notification in our private staff server when a new order or message comes in. It includes what you wrote, and Discord handles it under the Discord Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub hosts this website. GitHub records information about visits, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts provides this site's fonts. Your browser asks Google for them, so Google receives your IP address under the Google Privacy Policy.

How we use it

We use your order details and messages to quote, build, test, deliver and support your bot, and to contact you about your order. We don't sell your information or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your order and chat while we may need them to support your bot. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Orders placed before October 21, 2026 were posted as public issues in our GitHub repository. Ask us and we'll delete yours.

Your choices

  • Leave the contact field empty. You can still talk to us in your chat.
  • Ask us in your chat to delete your order. We'll delete the order and every message in it.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For questions, message us in your order chat.
