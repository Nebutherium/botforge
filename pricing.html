How an order goes

Every order gets its own private chat with us. That’s where you get your quote, see the status and message the team. No account needed.

  1. Send your order

    Fill out the form below. Your private chat with us opens right away, with your order in it.

  2. Get a quote

    We send a price and a date in your chat. Press Accept when it looks right. You pay once you accept.

  3. We build it

    A developer picks it up and your order moves to In progress.

  4. Try it

    We test it, then you try it in your own server.

  5. Go live

    You sign off and it's yours, hosted by us if you want.
