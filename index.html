    Ask on Discord
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          How do I place an order?
          
    Fill out the order form and copy your brief. Then join our Discord server, open an order ticket, and paste your brief. We'll send your quote in the ticket.
    
        
    
        
    
          How long does a bot take?
          
    Simple bots can take 2–3 days. Your quote includes an estimated completion date based on what you ask for.
    
        
    
        
    
          Do I have to host the bot myself?
          
    No. We can host it for you for $7 a month, so there's nothing to download or keep running. If you'd rather host it yourself, ask us about getting the source.
    
        
    
        
    
          Can I change the bot after it launches?
          
    Yes. Message us with what you'd like changed and we'll quote it before starting.
    
        
    
        
    
          What do you need from me to get started?
          
    The bot's name, what it should do, any commands you already have in mind, your Minecraft server if there is one, and your budget.
