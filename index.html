06 / Support

Questions before you order?

Quick answers are below. If yours isn't here, ask us on Discord and we'll help.

Ask on Discord
How do I place an order?

Fill out the order form on this page and copy your brief. Then join our Discord, click Order a bot in our Order Center, and paste your brief into the ticket it opens. We'll send your quote right there.

When do I pay?

Only after you approve your quote. Filling out the form and opening a ticket costs nothing.

How long does a bot take?

Simple bots can take 2–3 days. Your quote includes an estimated completion date based on what you ask for.

Do I have to host the bot myself?

No. We can host it for you for $7 a month, so there's nothing to download or keep running. If you'd rather host it yourself, ask us about getting the source.

Can you build something that isn't listed?

Yes. Choose Fully custom, describe your idea in the order form, and we'll scope it with you in your ticket.

Can I change the bot after it launches?

Yes. Message us with what you'd like changed and we'll quote it before starting.

What do you need from me to get started?

The bot's name, what it should do, any commands you already have in mind, your Minecraft server if there is one, and your budget.
